# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday online shoppers in Pakistan comparing prices across local stores (Daraz, iShopping, Telemart, etc.) before buying. They arrive with intent — a product in mind or a link in hand — and want to know, quickly and without noise, where it is cheapest and whether the price is good right now. Many arrive on a phone. They are price-conscious, slightly skeptical of "deal" sites, and reward a tool that feels honest and gets out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Bhao.pk is an intelligent price-comparison engine: search or paste a URL, and it aggregates live listings across vendors, ranks them, tracks history, and lets users save products and set price alerts. It stores no product catalog of its own — prices are live. Success is a user trusting the number on the screen enough to click through and buy, and coming back to track the next thing.

## Brand Personality

Effortless, calm, quietly premium. Three words: **considered, honest, timeless.** The voice is plain and confident — no hype, no exclamation marks, no "MEGA DEALS." It should feel like a well-made piece of stationery or a fine retail experience: warm, spacious, unhurried. Trust is the emotional goal; the interface earns it by being clear rather than loud.

## Anti-references

- **The current bhao.pk** — acid-lime neon on near-black, uppercase-everything, monospace body text, "hacker terminal" energy. Reads cheap and mechanical.
- Glowing/neon accents, dark gamer UIs, RGB gradients, "cyber" aesthetics.
- Aggressive discount-marketplace styling (Daraz/Temu): loud reds, countdown timers, badge spam, cramped grids.
- Generic cold SaaS: pure-white + electric-blue + Inter-everything with no warmth or point of view.

## Design Principles

1. **Effortless over engineered.** Premium comes from restraint, space, and typography — never from effects, glows, or ornament. If a flourish announces itself, remove it.
2. **Type and space carry the weight.** Since product imagery is scraped and inconsistent, hierarchy, rhythm, and generous whitespace do the persuading. The layout is the luxury.
3. **Honest by default.** Show the real number, the real store, the real saving. No manufactured urgency. Semantic color is earned, not decorative.
4. **Mobile is not an afterthought.** Every surface is designed for the thumb first — reachable actions, real tap targets, layouts that are native to small screens rather than shrunk desktop.
5. **Calm consistency.** One button vocabulary, one card, one set of states across every screen. Delight lives in moments (a smooth reveal, a considered empty state), not on every pixel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body/UI text meets 4.5:1, large text and UI affordances meet 3:1, in both light and dark themes.
- Full light and dark modes, honoring system preference with a manual override that persists.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — motion is enhancement, never required to understand state.
- Visible, non-color focus states on every interactive element; never rely on color alone to convey meaning (saving, in/out of stock, alert direction).
- Real tap targets (≥44px) on mobile.
